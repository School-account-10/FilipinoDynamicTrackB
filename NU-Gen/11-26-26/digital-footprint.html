Digital Footprint

Everything you do online leaves a trail. Learn how to shape one you are proud of.

Two kinds of footprint

Active footprint

What you choose to share.

  • Posts, comments, and photos
  • Videos and stories
  • Reviews and forum replies
  • Forms you fill in

Passive footprint

What is collected as you browse.

  • Sites and searches
  • Location data
  • Tags others add of you
  • Cookies and ad tracking

Where a single post can end up

Posted

You share it with a small group.

Saved

Someone screenshots or reposts it.

Found

It becomes searchable or spreads.

Seen

Future schools, employers, or scholarship panels may view it.

Deleting a post does not guarantee every copy is gone. The best protection is to think before posting.

A five-minute footprint check

  • Search your own name and see what appears
  • Review old public posts and photos
  • Check who can see your profile
  • Remove tags you do not want
  • Update your bio and profile photo for a professional look

Myths vs. facts

Myth: Private accounts are completely private.
Fact: Anyone in your circle can screenshot or share.
Myth: I can delete it and it is gone.
Fact: Copies and archives may remain.
Myth: Only what I post counts.
Fact: Tags, comments, and data collected about you also count.
