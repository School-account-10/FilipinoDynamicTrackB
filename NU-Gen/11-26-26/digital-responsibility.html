Digital Responsibility

Be accountable for your digital actions and make informed decisions that you can stand behind.

Pause. Think. Post.

Five quick checks before you send, share, or comment.

Is it true?

Have I checked it?

Is it kind?

Would it hurt someone?

Is it mine to share?

Do I have permission?

Is it safe?

Does it expose private info?

Can I own it?

Would I stand behind it later?

What accountability looks like

  • Admit mistakes and fix them quickly
  • Credit original creators
  • Correct misinformation you shared
  • Choose not to join harmful trends
  • Help others learn good habits

A Nationalian digital pledge

  • I will protect my information and respect others' privacy.
  • I will check before I share.
  • I will use technology and AI honestly.
  • I will speak up against harm online.
