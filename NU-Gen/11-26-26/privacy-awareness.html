Privacy Awareness

Understand consent, data privacy, and how to share information safely.

How sensitive is your information?

The more sensitive it is, the more carefully you should share it.

Lower risk

  • Display name or nickname
  • Public interests
  • School-approved announcements

Be careful

  • Full name and birthday
  • Photos of your home or school ID
  • Live location and daily routine

Protect strongly

  • Passwords and one-time codes
  • Government ID and bank details
  • Health or private family information

Ask before you click Allow

  • Why does this app need this permission?
  • Does it need my contacts, camera, or location to work?
  • Who will receive and keep my data?
  • Can I say no and still use the basics?

Consent goes both ways

Respect others' privacy

  • Ask before posting a photo of someone
  • Do not share private chats or documents
  • Blur or crop names and faces when needed
  • Tag only with permission

Good to know: In the Philippines, the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) protects personal information. Ask the National Privacy Commission or your school for details about your rights.

Common mistake

Accepting every app permission without reading.

Common mistake

Posting a boarding pass, school ID, or ticket photo with its barcode visible.

Common mistake

Using the same personal details for every sign-up.
