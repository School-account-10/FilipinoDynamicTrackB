Building a More Responsible Digital Generation.

NU-Gen is a student-developed concept under the Responsible Digital Nationalian track at National University Las Piñas. It responds to a growing concern: students spend much of their day online, but not everyone has the guidance to stay safe, think critically, and communicate with care.

MisinformationCyberbullyingOnline scamsPrivacy and data protectionResponsible AI useDigital etiquetteDigital footprintsEthical communication

Why it matters

Digital citizenship protects people and communities. Small online choices add up to a safer, more respectful campus.

Who it is for

Nationalians first, and the wider community who share the same digital spaces.

The proposed solution

A friendly hub with a digital guide, learning previews, university information, and a space for student suggestions.

Expected impact

Students who recognize risks, act responsibly, and help build respectful online communities.

Know Your University.

Official statements are taken from the National University website.

NU Vision

We are National University, a dynamic private institution committed to nation building, recognized internationally in teaching and research.

NU Mission

Guided by the core values and characterized by our cultural heritage of Dynamic Filipinism, National University is committed to providing relevant, innovative, and accessible quality education and other development programs.

  • Students by molding them into ethical, spiritual and responsible citizens.
  • Faculty and employees by enhancing their competencies, cultivating their commitment and providing a just and fulfilling work environment.
  • Alumni by instilling in them a sense of pride, commitment, and loyalty to their alma mater.
  • Industry partners and employers by providing them Nationalians who will contribute to their growth and development.
  • Community by contributing to the improvement of life's conditions.
